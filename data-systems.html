CHANNEL 2 · UNIVERSITIES

University outreach pipeline

Universities were a brand-new channel for us, with no list, no process, and no CRM. I designed the system end to end, documented it as a playbook in Notion, and ran it over the spring and summer of 2026.

Process flow

1. Source
Find the right schools (small private 4-year, 1K–5K students) and the person who owns student success
2. Enrich
Clay + LinkedIn data: title, tenure, and whether they're still at the school
3. Verify & score
Verify the email; score fit, with current-employer match as the gate; check the 839-school do-not-contact list
4. Sequence
Instantly: first email plus up to three follow-ups, with AI-assisted personal first lines
5. Handle replies
Positive-reply protocol: book an intro call fast
6. Pipeline
Notion tracker: stage, likelihood, notes, and next step for every school
7. Close
Security review (HECVAT) → pilot agreement

A 3.8% reply rate is ordinary for cold email. Conversion after the first conversation was the strong part: about 1 in 7 cold-sourced schools that entered the pipeline signed a pilot, selling into higher education, a market known for slow buying cycles. Signed pilots include the University of Louisville, University of Dallas, Pfeiffer University, and Bryan College.

Logical data model

The system ran on Notion databases synced with Instantly. Logically, it follows this structure:

Pipeline stage definitions

StageDefinition
Pre-MeetingContacted or introduced; no call yet
Meeting BookedIntro call scheduled
Post-Intro MeetingCall held; evaluating fit
LegalSecurity review and agreement in progress
Agreement SignedPilot live
No Further ActionClosed: no fit, no budget, or went silent

Keeping the data clean

  • School-first sourcing: start from the school and find who owns student success there. About 71% of schools had a clear owner.
  • Freshness gate: contacts only scored if their current employer matched the school, which filtered out people who'd changed jobs.
  • Do-not-contact list of 839+ schools that were out of scope or already in conversation, so no school got emailed twice by mistake.

Diagnosing performance

My playbook breaks weak results into causes and tests one variable at a time:

Low positive repliesOffer, copy, or listChange one: offer, subject and first line, or targeting
Replies but few meetingsWeak next stepTighter call-to-action; better follow-up
Replies from the wrong peopleList quality or positioningRefine fit filters; reword pain points
No responses at allDeliverability or volumeDomain warm-up, inbox placement, send volume
