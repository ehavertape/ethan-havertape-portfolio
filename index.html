About me

I'm finishing my B.S.B.A. in Computer Information Systems at the University of Louisville. Through UofL's Sandbox program, I spent a large share of my degree building Due Gooder full-time, so my capstone-level work comes from a real company with real users and university customers.

At Due Gooder I own go-to-market: marketing, sales, partnerships, and business operations. Due Gooder grew consumer-first, selling directly to students, and I ran our fall creator campaign with Vizi Labs (50 creators, 5,200+ posts, 17.9 million views). I also opened universities as a new sales channel: I built our outreach system from scratch and co-led our security review with the University of Louisville. Outside Due Gooder, I work part-time as a GTM Engineer at PayFWDs, a payroll and HR software company, where I own audience building, funnel tracking, and automation.

This portfolio pairs my CIS coursework (database design, programming, web development, and statistics) with that professional work. Each section explains which skill it demonstrates and why it matters.
