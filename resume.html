Cover letter

November 2026
Hiring Team, Revenue Operations
Filevine · 1 S Wacker Dr, Chicago, IL 60606

Dear Hiring Team,

I'm applying for the Revenue Operations Analyst role. Since co-founding Due Gooder in 2025, I've been doing a version of this job at a company small enough that I had to build every piece myself, and I'm looking to bring that to a team where RevOps is a real function.

As co-founder and COO of Due Gooder, an AI study platform with 320,000+ student users, I own go-to-market. We grew consumer-first, and this year I opened universities as a new sales channel, building the pipeline from scratch. I sourced and enriched more than 1,000 contacts across 129 schools, set up a scoring rubric and do-not-contact rules to keep the data clean, and ran sequenced outreach that turned into four signed university pilots. When reply rates were low, I worked out whether the problem was the list, the copy, or the offer, and tested one variable at a time. That's the same funnel-bottleneck work your posting describes. On the consumer side, I ran our fall creator campaign with Vizi Labs: 50 creators, 5,228 posts, and 17.9 million views. I had our analytics intern break the results down by hook type and platform, and used it to decide what to change for next semester.

I also co-led our HECVAT security assessment with the University of Louisville. That meant translating hundreds of technical questions into answers a university security office could approve, which is close to the "plain English for stakeholders" skill you list. Part-time at PayFWDs, a payroll and HR software company, I own audience building, funnel tracking, and automation for the sales team.

My CIS degree with a Data Analytics concentration backs this up: relational database design in SQL Server, statistical analysis in Excel and SPSS, and enough programming to work comfortably alongside technical teams. I'm used to learning tools fast. Salesforce, HubSpot, and Gong would be new to me on paper, but the data problems underneath them aren't.

I'd welcome the chance to talk about how I can help your team move deals faster with cleaner data.

Thanks,
Ethan Havertape
